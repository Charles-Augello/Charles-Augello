Facilitating collaborative design thinking to discover and solidify product strategy

Multiple design thinking workshops were facilitated over an 18-month period to rethink home lending from the customer’s perspective and develop a strategy for a refreshed end-to-end experience.

About my product design facilitation process

A series of design thinking workshops, based on earlier home ownership user research and co-facilitated by designers across my team, resulted in a product strategy for an enhanced home-lending experience. My focus was creating concepts for a streamlined application experience and facilitating design thinking activities to improve the hardship servicing experience.
