About my UX design process

A year of discovery, including user interviews, observations and task analysis, identified and clarified needs and points of pain. That was followed by validated customer journey mapping, conceptual ideation, and a three-year iterative agile implementation. The result was a state-of-the-art experience built on new technologies and an industry-leading product.
