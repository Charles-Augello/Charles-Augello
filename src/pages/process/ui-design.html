Presenting complex information in a usable format

The business needed to display an extensive list of fund attributes in a format that was equally usable and efficient for investment professionals and novices.

About my UI design process

I started from user research findings that revealed the mental models of a diverse spectrum of users. Iterative exercises then organized hundreds of data attributes into a meaningful structure. I led the design of the display, and that structure has withstood the test of time, helping investors of all levels understand individual offerings for over twenty years.
