Home/Process/User research

Understanding the thoughts, emotions and desires on the journey to home ownership

A six-month facilitated research study to understand how mortgage customers approach obtaining home ownership, from the borrower’s perspective.

About my user research discovery process

Time was set aside each week to interview a cross section of homeowners to learn what mattered to them and how they navigated their personal journey to home ownership.

What we learned became the basis for articulating the user perspective throughout the design process, and it became a framework for organizing products.
